SOAL EVALUASI DETERMINAN DAN INVERS#

A. Hitunglah determinan matrik berikut dengan menggunakan rumus expansi baris#

\[\sum_{k=1}^n (-1)^{i+k} a_{ik} M_{ik}\]

dengan \(M_{ij}\) adalah minior dari matrik A dan

\[ M_{ij} = \det A_{ij}. \]

\(A_{ij}\) adalah submatrik dengan menghapus baris i dan kolom kolom j dari matrix \(A_{mxn}\) dengan \(1 \le i, j \le n\)

  1. \[\begin{split}A = \begin{bmatrix} -7 & -5 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
  2. \[\begin{split} A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & -3 \\ 1 & -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
  3. \[\begin{split}A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix}.\end{split}\]

JAWABAN#

A. Determinan Matriks (Ekspansi Baris)#

Determinan matriks dapat dihitung dengan ekspansi baris:

\[ \det(A) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (-1)^{i+k} a_{ik} M_{ik} \]

dengan:

  • \(M_{ik}\) = minor (determinan submatriks hasil menghapus baris \(i\) dan kolom \(k\))

  • \(C_{ik} = (-1)^{i+k} M_{ik}\) = kofaktor

1. Matriks 2×2#

Diketahui: $\( A = \begin{bmatrix} -7 & -5 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \)$

Ekspansi baris pertama:

\[ \det(A) = (-1)^{1+1}(-7)M_{11} + (-1)^{1+2}(-5)M_{12} \]

Hitung minor:

\[ M_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} 4 \end{vmatrix} = 4 \]
\[ M_{12} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1 \]

Hitung kofaktor:

\[ C_{11} = (+1)(4) = 4, \quad C_{12} = (-1)(1) = -1 \]

Substitusi:

\[ \det(A) = (-7)(4) + (-5)(-1) \]
\[ = -28 + 5 = -23 \]

Hasil: $\( \det(A) = -23 \)$

2. Matriks 3×3#

Diketahui:

\[\begin{split}A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix}.\end{split}\]

Ekspansi baris pertama:

\[ \det(A) = \sum_{k=1}^{3} (-1)^{1+k} a_{1k} M_{1k} \]
\[ = 0\cdot C_{11} + 2\cdot C_{12} + (-3)\cdot C_{13} \]

Hitung minor#

\[\begin{split} M_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = (-2)(1) - (-1)(0) = -2 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{12} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = (1)(1) - (-1)(0) = 1 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{13} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = (1)(0) - (-2)(0) = 0 \end{split}\]

Hitung kofaktor#

\[ C_{11} = (+1)(-2) = -2 \]
\[ C_{12} = (-1)(1) = -1 \]
\[ C_{13} = (+1)(0) = 0 \]

Substitusi#

\[ \det(A) = 0(-2) + 2(-1) + (-3)(0) \]
\[ = -2 \]

Hasil: $\( \det(A) = -2 \)$

3. Matriks 4×4 (Ekspansi Lengkap)#

Diketahui: $\( A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} \)$

Ekspansi baris pertama:

\[ \det(A) = 1\cdot C_{11} . (-3)\cdot C_{12} . 1\cdot C_{13} . 1\cdot C_{14} \]

Minor \(M_{11}\)#

\begin{aligned} &= 1 \begin{vmatrix} -3 & 1 \ 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix}

  • 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \ 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix}

  • 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -3 \ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \ &= 1(9 - 1) - (-3 - 1) + (1 + 3) \ &= 8 + 4 + 4 = 16 \end{aligned}

Minor \(M_{12}\)#

\[\begin{split} M_{12} = \begin{vmatrix} -3 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = -3 \begin{vmatrix} -3 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix} - 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix} + 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[ = -3(9 - 1) - ( -3 - 1 ) + (1 + 3) \]
\[ = -24 + 4 + 4 = -16 \]

Minor \(M_{13}\)#

\[\begin{split} M_{13} = \begin{vmatrix} -3 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split}= -3 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix} - 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{vmatrix} + 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[ = -3(-3 - 1) - (-3 - 1) + (1 - 1) \]
\[ = 12 + 4 + 0 = 16 \]

Minor \(M_{14}\)#

\[\begin{split} M_{14} = \begin{vmatrix} -3 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} = -3 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} - 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} + 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} \end{split}\]
\[ = -3(1 + 3) - (1 + 3) + (1 - 1) \]
\[ = -12 - 4 + 0 = -16 \]

Kofaktor#

\[ C_{11} = (+1)(16) = 16 \]
\[ C_{12} = (-1)(-16) = 16 \]
\[ C_{13} = (+1)(16) = 16 \]
\[ C_{14} = (-1)(-16) = 16 \]

Substitusi akhir#

\[ \det(A) = 1(16) + (-3)(16) + 1(16) + 1(16) \]
\[ = 16 - 48 + 16 + 16 \]
\[ = 0 \]

B. Gunakan rumus matriks adjoin untuk menghitung invers dari matriks berikut dengan rumus#

\[ (\operatorname{adj} A)_{ij} = (-1)^{i+j} M_{ji} \]

dan

\[ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det A} \operatorname{adj} A. \]
  1. \[\begin{split}A = \begin{bmatrix} -7 & -5 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
  2. \[\begin{split}A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & -3 \\ 1 & -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
  3. \[\begin{split}A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix}.\end{split}\]

JAWABAN SOAL B#

B. Invers Matriks (Metode Adjoin)#

4. Matriks 2×2#

Diketahui: $\( A = \begin{bmatrix} -7 & -5 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \)$

Determinan#

\[ \det(A) = (-7)(4) - (-5)(1) \]
\[ = -28 + 5 = -23 \]

Minor#

\[ M_{11} = \begin{vmatrix} 4 \end{vmatrix} = 4 \]
\[ M_{12} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1 \]
\[ M_{21} = \begin{vmatrix} -5 \end{vmatrix} = -5 \]
\[ M_{22} = \begin{vmatrix} -7 \end{vmatrix} = -7 \]

Kofaktor#

Gunakan: $\( C_{ij} = (-1)^{i+j} M_{ij} \)$

\[ C_{11} = (+1)(4) = 4 \]
\[ C_{12} = (-1)(1) = -1 \]
\[ C_{21} = (-1)(-5) = 5 \]
\[ C_{22} = (+1)(-7) = -7 \]

Matriks Kofaktor#

\[\begin{split} C = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -1 \\ 5 & -7 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Adjoin#

\[ adj(A)= A^T \]
\[\begin{split} Mengubah baris menjadi kolom dan kolom menjadi baris \text{adj}(A) = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ -1 & -7 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Invers#

\[ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \cdot \text{adj}(A) \]
\[\begin{split} = \frac{1}{-23} \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ -1 & -7 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hasil Akhir#

\[\begin{split} A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac{4}{23} & -\frac{5}{23} \\ \frac{1}{23} & \frac{7}{23} \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

5. Matriks 3×3#

Diketahui: $\( A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & -3 \\ 1 & -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \)$

Substitusi Determinan#

\[ \det(A) = 0(-2) + 2(-1) + (-3)(0) \]
\[ = -2 \]

Minor Lengkap (untuk invers)#

\[\begin{split} M_{21} = \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 2 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{22} = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -3 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 0 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{23} = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = 0 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{31} = \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ -2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = -8 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{32} = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -3 \\ 1 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = 3 \end{split}\]
\[\begin{split} M_{33} = \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & -2 \end{vmatrix} = -2 \end{split}\]

Matriks Kofaktor#

Gunakan pola tanda: $$ \begin{bmatrix}

  • & - & + \

  • & + & - \

  • & - & + \end{bmatrix} $$

\[\begin{split} C = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -1 & 0 \\ -2 & 0 & 0 \\ -8 & -3 & -2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Adjoin#

\[\begin{split} \text{adj}(A) = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -2 & -8 \\ -1 & 0 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & -2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Invers#

\[ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \cdot \text{adj}(A) \]
\[\begin{split} = \frac{1}{-2} \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -2 & -8 \\ -1 & 0 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & -2 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Hasil Akhir#

\[\begin{split} A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 4 \\ \frac{1}{2} & 0 & \frac{3}{2} \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

6. Matriks 4×4#

Diketahui: $\( A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} \)$

Determinan#

Diketahui: $\( \det(A) = 0 \)$

Kesimpulan#

Karena: $\( \det(A) = 0 \)$

maka matriks bersifat singular, sehingga:

\[ A^{-1} (Invers) \text{ tidak ada} \]
